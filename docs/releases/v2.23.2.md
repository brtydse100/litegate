# LiteGate v2.23.2

## Highlights

- **Keys can no longer be created around LiteGate:** People are now provisioned
  in LiteLLM with the view-only `internal_user_viewer` role. They can still use
  their keys and see their own keys and spend, but can no longer create keys
  without LiteGate's budgets and policies through the LiteLLM Admin UI or
  `/key/generate`.
- **Existing people are switched automatically:** People with the plain
  `internal_user` role are moved to the viewer role at sign-in and once at
  startup. Each switch is audited as `litellm.user_role_restricted`.
  Administrators and other roles are unchanged.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Also lock down LiteLLM.** People who never sign in through LiteGate keep
  their LiteLLM role. Restrict key creation to administrators in LiteLLM's own
  `config.yaml` and restart LiteLLM:

  ```yaml
  litellm_settings:
    key_generation_settings:
      personal_key_generation:
        allowed_user_roles: ["proxy_admin"]
      team_key_generation:
        allowed_team_member_roles: ["admin"]
  ```

- No new LiteGate settings, migrations or dependencies.

**Details:** [Lock down key creation in LiteLLM](../litellm-key-lockdown.md).

**Changes:** [Provision people as LiteLLM viewers so keys cannot be minted around LiteGate](https://github.com/brtydse100/litegate/pull/68) by @brtydse100.
