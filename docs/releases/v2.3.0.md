# LiteGate v2.3.0

## Highlights

- **Team members:** Administrator member lists and destination search with explicit policy confirmation.
- **Moves:** Preserve the member's LiteLLM role and move all source-team keys through the portal or `POST /api/v1/teams/{source_team_id}/members/move`.
- **Safety:** Add destination membership, move and verify keys, then remove source membership; failure retains the source membership.

## Upgrade Notes

- No database migration or new required setting.
- Moves support up to 500 source-team keys. Update `oidc_group_team_mapping`/`KEY_TEAM_ID` before moving members out of referenced teams.
- Ordinary users cannot list or move team members.
