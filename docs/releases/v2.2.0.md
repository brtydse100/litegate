## What's Changed

LiteGate 2.2 adds administrator team management to the portal and API.

- Added an administrator-only **Teams** tab with paginated search.
- Added team creation with model, budget, reset, TPM/RPM, and blocked policy.
- Added quick policy editing without exposing the LiteLLM master key to the
  browser.
- Added guarded team deletion and matching `/api/v1/teams` automation routes.

### Safety

Normal users cannot see the Teams tab or call its API routes. Deletion requires
the exact team ID because LiteLLM also deletes the team's scoped keys. LiteGate
refuses deletion while a team is referenced by `oidc_group_team_mapping` or
`KEY_TEAM_ID`.

### Upgrade

No LiteGate database migration or new required setting is introduced. The
connected LiteLLM deployment must provide the paginated `/v2/team/list`
management endpoint.

See [team management](../features.md#administrator-only-team-management) and the
[Teams API](../../API.md#teams-admin-only).
