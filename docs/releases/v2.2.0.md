# LiteGate v2.2.0

## Highlights

- **Teams:** Administrator-only paginated search, creation and policy editing for models, budgets, reset periods, TPM/RPM and blocked state.
- **Automation:** Guarded deletion and matching `/api/v1/teams` routes; the LiteLLM master key stays out of the browser.

## Upgrade Notes

- No database migration or new required setting. LiteLLM must provide `/v2/team/list`.
- Deletion requires the exact team ID and also deletes its scoped keys. Teams referenced by `oidc_group_team_mapping` or `KEY_TEAM_ID` cannot be deleted.
- Ordinary users cannot access team administration.
