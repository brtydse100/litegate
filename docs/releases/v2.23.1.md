# LiteGate v2.23.1

## Highlights

- **Browser-tab icon:** LiteGate now shows its mark on the browser tab. When
  `logo_url` is set, the tab shows your organization's logo instead.
- **Cleaner custom logos:** A configured logo fills the same rounded square
  tile as the LiteGate mark in the sidebar and on the sign-in page, instead of
  sitting in a white framed pill. It looks the same in light and dark themes.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Use a square logo.** The logo is now cropped to a square tile, so a wide
  wordmark loses its edges. A square image with its own background fits best.
- No new settings, migrations or dependencies.
